# 06 · Disclosure statements

*The Massive AI Manual — Student Papers guide*

Honesty is hard when it's vague. Make it easy: one clear sentence that says
exactly what AI did in your work. This guide gives you a template, graded
examples, and placement rules.

---

## The core template

Whenever AI touched the work in any allowed way, add a line like this:

> **AI use statement:** I used [tool] to [what it did]. All ideas, arguments,
> and final text are my own. [Sources were located and read by me.]

That sentence covers almost every green- and yellow-zone use in
[guide 01](01-what-counts-as-cheating.md). Everything below is variations on it.

## Graded examples, with verdicts

**🟢 Tutoring only (explanations, quizzes, practice):**

> *"I used an AI chatbot to explain the concept of 'lead time' and to quiz me
> on my own draft. No drafting or editing was done by AI. All writing and
> sourcing is my own."*

**🟢 Feedback loop (critique you acted on):**

> *"I used [tool] for feedback on the argument structure of my draft. I
> revised the paper myself in response to that feedback. Ideas, evidence,
> and wording are entirely my own."*

**🟡 Outlining help (where the school allows it):**

> *"I used [tool] to brainstorm possible arguments and to identify weak spots
> in an outline I wrote myself. The final outline, all writing, and all sources
> are my own work."*

**🟡 Authorized AI-assisted editing (where the school explicitly allows it):**

> *"Per your permission on [date], I used [tool] to suggest grammar and
> rewording fixes to my own draft; I accepted individual suggestions and
> reworded the final text myself."*

**🔴 Not a disclosure (these do NOT count):**

- "Some AI was used." — Vague = not disclosed.
- "My friend used AI to check my paper." (Third-party AI use in your paper
  is still your disclosure to make.)
- Verbal-only disclosure in office hours. (Also make it written.)
- Disclosure added *after* the teacher questions you. (Too late — the point
  of disclosure is that it precedes discovery.)

## Placement rules

1. **Preferred: end of the document** — after the references, titled
   "AI Use Statement." One paragraph, nothing hidden.
2. **Accepted: in the submission notes** — the comment box on your LMS,
   assignment portal, or email to the teacher.
3. **Required where stated: wherever your teacher asked** — some want it in
   a header, some want a separate form. Follow the syllabus
   ([guide 02](02-school-policy-translation.md)).

Default to #1: a statement inside the document can never be misplaced.

## What to disclose — granularity guide

Disclose the *category* and *scope*, not a chat log:

| Use | Disclose? | How much |
|-----|-----------|----------|
| AI explained a concept during research | Yes, one clause | "used for explanations during research" |
| AI quizzed you on your draft | Yes, one clause | "quizzed me on my own draft" |
| AI critiqued argument structure; you revised | Yes, one clause | "provided feedback I acted on myself" |
| Grammar checker on your own sentences | Usually counts as [editing, not AI writing](01-what-counts-as-cheating.md); mention it only if your school's policy names tools | fine |
| AI drafted text you submitted | Don't. If allowed, disclose fully & specifically; if unsure, get written permission first | full |
| AI suggested citations | Never submit AI-suggested citations unverified | see [guide 05](05-verifying-citations.md) |

The pattern: **disclose the role, don't dump the log.** "Used for feedback"
is plenty. Pasting a 40-message transcript is not required — and a
"used for feedback" that's really "AI drafted it" is the same as no disclosure
at all. The sentence must still be *true*.

## For teachers: a classroom disclosure sheet

Project or print this. Every student writes ONE sentence before submitting:

```
AI USE STATEMENT — [Assignment name]
Name: ____________________  Date: __________

1. Did you use any AI tool at any stage?         Yes / No
2. If yes, which tool(s): ________________________
3. What did it do?  (check all that apply)
   [ ] explained concepts
   [ ] helped brainstorm / outline
   [ ] critiqued my draft; I revised
   [ ] grammar/spelling suggestions
   [ ] suggested sources (verified by me before use)
   [ ] drafted text (with your written permission)
   [ ] other: ______________________
4. One-sentence summary of AI's role:
   ________________________________________________
5. Signature: ____________________
```

Sign it. Unchecked boxes mean the work is your own — honest answers only.
Collecting these each assignment makes AI use discussable and auditable,
and (this is the real goal) it makes the *dishonest* path require an actual
lie on paper, which is a much higher bar than silently borrowing a paragraph.

## What honest disclosure does NOT do

- It does not immunize cheating. Disclosure of red-zone use is a confession,
  not permission. Get permission *first* for red-zone plans.
- It does not replace reading sources ([guide 05](05-verifying-citations.md)).
- It does not mean "I can use AI for everything now." It means one specific
  role, described accurately.
