## The manual in one paragraph

AI can help you think better, or it can help you avoid thinking. The difference isn't
which tool you use — it's **who does the understanding**. If AI explains, questions,
quizzes, and criticizes while *you* read, decide, and write, you're learning. If AI
produces the paragraphs and you click submit, you're not learning, and you're probably
cheating. This manual shows you exactly where that line is, how to describe what AI
did in your work, and how to check that its facts and citations are real.
